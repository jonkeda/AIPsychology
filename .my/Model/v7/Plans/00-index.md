# V7 Plans — Index

Design documents describe what the model *is*. These documents describe what to *do* with it.

The governing constraint is stated once here and assumed everywhere else: **there is no budget for formal validation, and there will not be one.** Every plan in this folder is built for a single unfunded author working with freely available tools. Nothing here depends on a research grant, a university affiliation, an institutional review board, or paid participants.

That constraint is less limiting than it first appears, but it does force one strategic reversal, which is the subject of [01-way-forward.md](01-way-forward.md).

## One rule that binds all of these

Everything in this folder is downstream of a single test, stated in full in [02-use-without-validation.md](02-use-without-validation.md):

> If the model turns out to be wrong, what happens to the people it was applied to?

Where the answer is *a conversation was slightly misdirected*, the use is fine. Where the answer is *someone's pay, role, or job changed*, the use is not permitted regardless of how confident anyone feels. This is not a caveat attached to the end of the plans. It is the design constraint that shapes them.
