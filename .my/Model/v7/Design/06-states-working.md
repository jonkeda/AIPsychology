# Positions — Working and Exit

Seven positions across two families, plus the Advocacy overlay. The Pre-engagement, Stance, and Disruption families are in [05-states-early.md](05-states-early.md).

---

# Working family

Four positions describing people who are using AI as part of their work. The question in this family is not whether AI is used but what the use is doing to the person and to the work.

## CU — Calibrated Use

> *"It's good for this, it's useless for that, and I know which is which."*

The person's judgement about where AI helps roughly matches where it actually helps. They use it without much drama, do not think about it a great deal, and their estimate updates when the tool changes.

`CU` Calibrated Use is a working equilibrium rather than an achievement. V6 called it Understanding, which invited the reading that everything before it was misunderstanding and everything after it was progress. Neither is the claim.

**Entry:** from `OE` Open Evaluation when judgement settles. From `SB` Scope Boundary when the line is recognised as movable. From `IS` Identity Shock directly, rare. From `VR` Values Refusal when the concern is resolved. From `FC` First Contact directly at low stakes with hands-on prior knowledge. From `OL` Oversight Load when volume is brought back under control. From `SD` Structural Displacement in a new domain.

**Attributes:**

| Attribute | Values | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `origin` | `direct` \| `post-shock` \| `coerced` | `coerced` is the important one — behaviour looks calibrated because it was required, not because judgement changed |
| `calibration-basis` | `tested` \| `inherited` | Whether the person's estimate comes from their own testing or from what their environment told them |

**Coerced `CU` Calibrated Use deserves its own note.** When an organisation forces delegation above a person's ceiling, the visible result is compliance that resembles calibration. Nothing underneath has changed. The pattern surfaces later as `GE` Guarded Evaluation, `OU` Overuse: mandate, or `BO` Burnout, typically months after everyone concluded the rollout had succeeded. It is the most common way organisations manufacture the outcome they were trying to avoid.

**Exits:** `OU` Overuse when volume outruns judgement. `OL` Oversight Load when review volume rises. `IP` Integrated Practice when the practice stabilises and becomes part of how the person works. `IS` Identity Shock as a regression under capability shock. `GE` Guarded Evaluation as a regression under access or trust shock. `DX` Disengagement.

**What an observer can see:** selective use with reasons that track the tool's actual reliability. The reasons are the signal, not the selectivity. `SB` Scope Boundary, `IP` Integrated Practice: mastery, and `CU` Calibrated Use all present as selective use.

**Conjecture —** `CU` Calibrated Use has been claimed to require two components: an internal recalibration and a social normalisation in the person's environment. **Testable —** place people classified `CU` Calibrated Use into environments where AI use is stigmatised. If a meaningful proportion regress to `SB` Scope Boundary or `GE` Guarded Evaluation without any change in their internal assessment, the social component is load-bearing.

## OU — Overuse

> *"I just run everything through it now."*

Use at a volume beyond what the person's own judgement supports. The definition is relative to judgement, not to a quantity — heavy use by someone who has accurately assessed that it works is not `OU` Overuse. Heavy use accompanied by declining verification is.

V6 split this into four positions differing only by motive. V7 keeps one position and records the motive as a driver, because the observable behaviour is the same in all four cases and only the sustaining mechanism differs.

**Entry:** from `CU` Calibrated Use when volume outruns judgement. From `FC` First Contact directly in mandated-adoption environments.

**Attributes:**

| Attribute | Values | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `driver` | `reward`, `anxiety`, `efficiency`, `social`, `mandate` | Multi-select, ordered by dominance. Combinations are the normal case |
| `duration` | `acute` \| `sustained` \| `chronic` | Weeks, months, years |
| `awareness` | `unaware` \| `partial` \| `aware-and-choosing` | `aware-and-choosing` is not necessarily a problem and should not be treated as one |

**Drivers:**

| Driver | What sustains it | Removing it does what | V6 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `reward` | Intermittent good outputs sustain use regardless of the failure rate | Usage drops when the reward schedule flattens | `S6E` |
| `anxiety` | Self-trust has transferred to the tool; the person no longer trusts their unaided judgement | Removing the tool produces distress out of proportion to the task | `S6D` |
| `efficiency` | Throughput targets override quality judgement | Usage drops when tracking stops | `S6R` |
| `social` | Peer adoption and visibility drive use | Usage differs sharply between solo and observed work | `S6S` |
| `mandate` | Use is a condition of the role, measured and enforced | Usage drops immediately when the mandate lifts | **New in V7** |

`mandate` is separated from `efficiency` because the psychology is different. Under `efficiency` the person has internalised the target and is driving themselves. Under `mandate` they have not, and the resulting position is closer to compliance than to motivation. **New in V7 —** the separation is untested, and the two are hard to tell apart in environments where the mandate has been in place long enough to feel normal.

**Exits:** `IP` Integrated Practice when the driver runs dry or fails. `OL` Oversight Load when delegation moves to D5 and review becomes the work. `BO` Burnout when cost exceeds value. `DX` Disengagement.

**What an observer can see:** volume, reliably. Driver, not at all. Volume without driver is close to useless for anything except spotting that a conversation is worth having.

**Probes by driver** — all indirect, because direct questions produce the socially acceptable answer:

| Driver | Probe |
| --- | --- |
| `anxiety` | *"If it were unavailable for a week — what would that feel like?"* Feel, not do. |
| `social` | *"If nobody could see how you worked, what would change?"* |
| `efficiency` | *"If throughput stopped being tracked tomorrow?"* |
| `reward` | *"When did it last surprise you badly?"* A `reward` driver has no specific answer. |
| `mandate` | *"If the requirement were lifted, what would you keep?"* |

`anxiety` is the hardest driver to detect and the most consequential. It is ego-threatening to report, and people reliably describe it as efficiency instead.

**Testable —** For each driver, removing the sustaining condition for 30 days should produce a substantial usage drop for that driver's group and not for the others. A driver whose removal changes nothing is not a driver.

## OL — Oversight Load

> *"I didn't write any of this. I'm responsible for all of it. I can't read it fast enough."*

**New in V7.** The person's work has become checking machine output rather than producing work, and the volume has reached or passed what they can check at the standard they hold.

`OL` Oversight Load is distinguished from `OU` Overuse by where the output comes from. In `OU` Overuse the person is generating the volume through their own delegation choices. In `OL` Oversight Load the volume arrives — from agents running on long horizons, from colleagues delegating upward, from a workflow redesigned around throughput — and the person owns the result without having watched it being made.

The characteristic experience is **responsibility without observation**. The person is accountable for work they did not see produced, at a volume that makes genuine review impossible, and they know it.

**Entry:** from `CU` Calibrated Use as review volume rises. From `OU` Overuse when delegation moves to D5. Directly from a workflow redesign, without passing through the rest of the model — this is the edge that makes `OL` Oversight Load awkward, because it can arrive as an organisational fact rather than a psychological trajectory.

**Attributes:**

| Attribute | Values | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `burden` | `substantial` \| `dominant` \| `exceeded` | Mirrors the Verification Burden axis in [03-axes.md](03-axes.md) |
| `response` | `rubber-stamping` \| `refusing` \| `standard-lowering` \| `redesigning` | What the person does when the burden exceeds capacity |
| `authorship` | `retained` \| `ambiguous` \| `lost` | Whether the person can locate their own contribution |
| `origin` | `self-delegated` \| `imposed` | Whether the person created the volume or received it |

**The four responses are the substance of the position:**

- **Rubber-stamping.** Approving what has not been adequately checked. Quality degrades invisibly and the person knows. **Conjecture —** knowing is the corrosive part, and this is a distinct experience from ordinary overwork.
- **Refusing.** Declining the volume, which registers as a productivity problem in any environment that measures throughput.
- **Standard-lowering.** Genuinely revising downward what counts as adequate. Sometimes correct; usually not deliberate.
- **Redesigning.** Rebuilding the workflow so review is sampled, staged, or automated. Routes to `IP` Integrated Practice: mastery. The only response that resolves rather than absorbs the problem.

**Exits:** `IP` Integrated Practice when review is redesigned. `CU` Calibrated Use when volume is brought back under control. `BO` Burnout when the burden stays exceeded. `DX` Disengagement.

**What an observer can see:** unusually well, compared to most of the model. The ratio of review time to production time is measurable from ordinary workflow data. What is not observable is the response — whether the person is rubber-stamping is exactly what they will not report.

**Why this is a position and not an attribute:** the objection is reasonable. `OL` Oversight Load could be `CU` Calibrated Use or `OU` Overuse with a high Verification Burden. It is proposed as a separate position because the exits differ: `OL` Oversight Load routes to `BO` Burnout through a mechanism that has nothing to do with the person's own usage patterns, and folding it into `OU` Overuse would attribute the load to the person's choices when it frequently is not theirs. This is a genuine open decision — see [10-decisions.md](10-decisions.md).

**Testable —** Verification Burden at `exceeded` should predict `BO` Burnout more strongly than raw usage volume does. Both are measurable from workflow data. If volume predicts as well or better, `OL` Oversight Load is not carrying its weight and should collapse into `OU` Overuse.

## IP — Integrated Practice

> *"This is just how I work now."*

AI is part of how the work gets done and part of how the person understands the work. The arrangement is stable, the person is not thinking about it much, and the question of whether to use AI has stopped being a question.

V6 split this into three positions differing only by style. V7 keeps one and records style as an attribute, because the three routinely occur together in the same person.

**Entry:** from `CU` Calibrated Use when practice stabilises. From `OU` Overuse when the driver runs dry or fails. From `OL` Oversight Load when review is redesigned. From `BO` Burnout on recovery.

**Attributes:**

| Attribute | Values | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `style` | `mastery`, `expanded`, `ethical` | Multi-select. Any combination, including all three |
| `stability` | `stable` \| `under-stress` | `under-stress` means approaching `BO` Burnout or a regression threshold |
| `ceiling` | `D1`–`D5` | Integrated practice with a low ceiling is entirely coherent and frequently missed |

**Styles:**

| Style | What it means | The discriminating observation | V6 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `mastery` | Knows where the tool works; the line moves on evidence | Boundary moves after testing, not after a release | `S7M` |
| `expanded` | Self-concept has grown to include what is now possible | Self-description changes, not just behaviour | `S7I` |
| `ethical` | Attribution, consent, and disclosure are built in | Can name a specific concern *and* a specific change made because of it | `S7E` |

`IP` Integrated Practice: mastery is worth stating plainly: it is **not** the goal of the model. It is one stable arrangement among several. A person in `SB` Scope Boundary with a firm line and a good career has not fallen short of it.

**Exits:** `BO` Burnout under sustained cost. `IS` Identity Shock as a regression under capability shock at high stakes. `GE` Guarded Evaluation as a regression under access or trust shock. `DX` Disengagement.

**Testable —** `IP` Integrated Practice: expanded should show measurably different professional self-description at 12 months compared to `IP` Integrated Practice: mastery, with AI incorporated into how the person describes what they are rather than only what they do. If self-descriptions do not diverge, `expanded` is not a distinct style.

**Testable —** `IP` Integrated Practice: ethical should be able to supply, in interview, a specific concern about their own use, a concrete change made or declined because of it, and a case where the answer remains genuinely unclear. Inability to supply all three across a majority of a classified sample would mean the style is picking up general discomfort rather than integrated ethical practice.

## AV — Advocacy (overlay)

> *"You have to try this. It changes everything."*

Not a position. A behaviour that runs on top of one — normally `IP` Integrated Practice, occasionally `OU` Overuse. A person is never in `AV` Advocacy; they are in `IP` Integrated Practice with `AV` Advocacy active.

V6 treated the equivalent as a state, which produced the incoherent implication that a person left Integration by starting to speak publicly.

**Attributes:**

| Attribute | Values | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `scope` | `peer` \| `organisational` \| `public` | |
| `grounding` | `practice-based` \| `enthusiasm-based` | `enthusiasm-based` without depth collapses under sustained questioning |
| `incentive` | `none` \| `role` \| `commercial` | Whether the advocacy is paid for. Changes how it should be read entirely |

**`AV` Advocacy over `OU` Overuse: reward** is the unstable combination. Advocacy grounded in enthusiasm rather than practice cannot absorb informed objection, and the person either retreats or hardens.

**Testable —** `AV` Advocacy grounded in prior `IS` Identity Shock experience is claimed to be more stable at 12 months than `AV` Advocacy without it, where stability means still advocating, no public reversal, and engaging substantive objections without deflection. Comparable stability rates would refute the depth claim.

# Exit family

Three positions sharing structural character: reached after engagement, long-residence, outside the working cycle, and resolved through structural change rather than psychological recalibration.

## BO — Burnout

> *"I don't want to look at any of it any more."*

Withdrawal after the cost of the arrangement exceeded its value. Exhaustion, not defeat — the person typically knows the tool works and cannot continue paying what it costs them.

**Entry:** from `OU` Overuse when cost exceeds value. From `OL` Oversight Load when the burden stays exceeded. From `IP` Integrated Practice under sustained cost.

**Attributes:**

| Attribute | Values | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `severity` | `mild` \| `moderate` \| `severe` | `severe` includes functional impairment; refer out |
| `trigger` | `cost-exceeded-value` \| `coerced-adoption` \| `verification-collapse` \| `capability-shock` \| `compounded` | `verification-collapse` is **New in V7 —** arriving via `OL` Oversight Load |
| `recovery` | `active` \| `passive` \| `absent` | `absent` predicts permanent `DX` Disengagement |

**Exits:** `IP` Integrated Practice on recovery. `DX` Disengagement, frequently permanent.

**Testable —** V6 routed all burnout through overuse. `BO` Burnout should be preceded by an identifiable `OU` Overuse or `OL` Oversight Load phase in a substantial majority of retrospective cases. A significant proportion with neither would mean the entry conditions are wrong and direct edges are missing.

## SD — Structural Displacement

> *"There isn't a version of this job left for me to do."*

The recognition that no viable position remains in the domain. Not a psychological position about identity — a structural conclusion about a labour market, reached alongside the identity problem rather than as its resolution.

`SD` Structural Displacement is legitimate and frequently correct. The model does not treat it as a failure of adaptation, and does not claim to know which roles are non-viable or when. That is an economics question the model cannot answer.

**Entry:** from `IS` Identity Shock when economic non-viability is confirmed. From `SB` Scope Boundary when the boundary erodes to nothing.

**Attributes:**

| Attribute | Values | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `domain-type` | `knowledge-work` \| `service-care-manual` | Knowledge work displaces by narrowing; service, care, and manual roles face potential elimination through a different mechanism the model does not describe |
| `resolution` | `occupational-transition` \| `principled-exit` \| `advocacy` | |
| `confirmation` | `feared` \| `partial` \| `confirmed` | `confirmed` means external evidence, not anticipation. The distinction is the whole difference between `SD` Structural Displacement and `IS` Identity Shock |

**Exits:** `CU` Calibrated Use in a new domain, which is a genuine and reasonably common outcome. `DX` Disengagement. Advocacy work outside the model's scope.

**Testable —** `SD` Structural Displacement should be distinguishable from high-practical-severity `DX` Disengagement by blind raters working from behavioural and interview evidence with stated reasons removed. Inter-rater agreement below roughly 0.6 would mean `SD` Structural Displacement is not reliably separable and should be demoted to a documented variant of `DX` Disengagement.

## DX — Disengagement

> *"I stopped bothering with it."*

The person engaged and then stopped. Promoted in V7 from a cross-cutting note to a named position, because "reachable from everywhere" describes an edge set and is not a reason to leave something unnamed.

`DX` Disengagement is not `NE` No Engagement — `NE` No Engagement never engaged. It is not `VR` Values Refusal — `VR` Values Refusal is a stance formed before engagement. It is not `SD` Structural Displacement — `SD` Structural Displacement is about viability, not willingness.

**Entry:** from every position in the model. The only universal edge.

**Attributes:**

| Attribute | Values | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `practical-severity` | 0–10 | How badly the tool failed the person's actual needs |
| `emotional-severity` | 0–10 | How much identity pressure or exhaustion drove the exit |
| `permanence` | `temporary` \| `permanent` | |
| `stated-reason` | free text | Recorded separately from the severities, because it frequently does not match them |

**Severity anchors:**

| Practical | | Emotional | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 | Tool met most needs; minor friction | 0–3 | Mild discomfort; self-concept intact |
| 4–6 | Real gaps; core tasks still worked | 4–6 | Meaningful identity pressure; confidence reduced |
| 7–10 | Failed critical tasks, or unusable | 7–10 | Severe identity disruption or exhaustion |

**The reversibility check** is the operational test that separates the two axes: supply a tool that solves the specific failure the person cited. If they restart, practical severity was driving. If they do not, emotional severity was driving and the practical reason was the acceptable thing to say.

**The two axes are independent, and the quadrants call for different responses:**

| | Low emotional | High emotional |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Low practical** | Often not worth intervening — opportunity cost or boredom | Support and gradual re-exposure; tools are not the issue |
| **High practical** | Better tooling, training, defaults | Both, in that order — fix the tool first, or the support reads as gaslighting |

**Conjecture —** inter-rater reliability of the 0–10 axes has never been established, and the anchors are the only thing standing between them and arbitrary numbers.

## A note on market-driven non-use

Some practitioners arrive at non-use *after* traversing the model, because their market rewards demonstrably AI-free work — handmade goods, AI-free therapeutic practice, regulated contexts where provenance is a requirement.

This is not `VR` Values Refusal, which forms before engagement. It is not `SB` Scope Boundary, because it is a whole-domain position rather than a line within one. It is not `DX` Disengagement in spirit, because nothing failed.

It is best recorded as `DX` Disengagement with a `stated-reason` of market positioning and both severities low. **New in V7 —** whether it needs its own position is an open decision in [10-decisions.md](10-decisions.md). The current judgement is that it does not, because the psychological content is thin — the person is making a business decision and is comfortable with it.
